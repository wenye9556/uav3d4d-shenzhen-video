# UAV 3D+4D Delivery Optimization — Shenzhen Case Video

60-second narrated walkthrough (English) of the optimized **7-UAV, 20-customer,
67-no-fly-zone** Shenzhen batch plan: **97.098 CNY**, zero separation
violations, certified optimality gap **4.32%** (global lower bound
**92.904 CNY**). Companion to manuscript **TRE-D-26-04139**.

## Files

- `uav3d4d_sz_60s_en.mp4` — English narration, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 60 s
  (a Chinese-narration copy is retained offline by the authors)
- `uav3d4d_sz_60s_delivery_manifest.json` — SHA-256, byte counts, fact source,
  QA summary

The video contains narration only; there is no music or sound effect.
Road and building backdrop: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); the case
layer is illustrative.

The certificate shown in the video reports the positive result: a 97.098 CNY
plan with a certified gap of 4.32% and zero separation violations. It does not
claim a proven optimum.
